# Azumio

> Azumio builds AI-driven digital health technology used by 100M+ people, and
> exposes it to developers through the Calorie Mama Food AI recognition API and
> related health/nutrition APIs and SDKs. The flagship developer product is the
> Calorie Mama Food Recognition API: submit a food photo, get back recognized
> foods with calories, macronutrients, and full nutrition.

## APIs

- [Calorie Mama Food Recognition API](https://dev.caloriemama.ai/docs): POST a JPEG image to /v1/foodrecognition (macros) or /v1/foodrecognition/full (full nutrition); API-key auth via user_key.
